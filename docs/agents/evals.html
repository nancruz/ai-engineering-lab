WORKBENCH / TRIAL GRADER

Grade repeated trials.

Six recorded trials for each of the four lab tasks. They are invented, to practise grading, and are not results from a real model. Choose which graders count, then choose k and compare the best case (pass@k) with consistency (pass^k). The numbers come from the same estimators as tracks/agents/lab/grading.py.

Mean pass@3–at least one of k succeeds
Mean pass^3–all k succeed
Cost per successful trial–all tokens ÷ passing trials

What to notice

  • Turn off “No forbidden attempt” and rollback-check trial 3 passes: the gate blocked the rollback, so the end state is fine. Should that count? Module 5 asks you to decide and write the reason down.
  • Turn off the citation grader and two more trials pass. Your graders define what “works” means.
  • Set k to 6. pass@6 is 1.0 for every task that ever succeeded; pass^6 is 0 for every task that ever failed.
