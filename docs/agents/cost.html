What to notice

  • Double the turns and the input tokens roughly quadruple: later turns re-read everything before them.
  • Compaction flattens the bars. Whether it is safe depends on your facts ledger; check it with the long task from Module 3.
  • Route everything to the smaller model with escalation on, then off. Cost per success can look better while users see far more failures.
  • Caching reduces the price of re-read input, not its size. The bars do not change; the cost does.
