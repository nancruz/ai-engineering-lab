# Engineering onboarding guide

Welcome to Lumen engineering. This guide covers your first three weeks. Your onboarding buddy will help with anything that is unclear.

## Week 1: access and setup

- Collect your laptop from IT and sign in with single sign-on.
- Request the `eng-all` access group in the access portal. Your manager approves it, usually the same day.
- Install the developer tools with `lumen dev setup` and start the local environment with `lumen dev up`.
- Clone your team's repositories and run their test suites once to make sure everything works.
- Join the team channel, the engineering announcements channel and the incidents channel.

## Week 2: first change

- Pick a small ticket labelled `good-first-change` with your buddy.
- Open a pull request; two reviews are needed for services in production.
- Deploy it through the pipeline with your buddy watching. Every new engineer ships to production in their second week.

## Week 3: operations

- Request the `oncall-shadow` access group.
- Shadow the on-call primary for a full week, following the on-call handbook.
- Read the runbooks for your team's services and the incident severity levels.

## Who to ask

- Access problems: the IT help channel.
- Pipeline or tooling problems: the developer experience team.
- Anything else: your buddy or your manager. Nobody expects you to know everything in your first month.
